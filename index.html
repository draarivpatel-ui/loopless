Model limits and search guarantees

The studio supports up to 12 errands. A completed Branch-and-Bound search is exact. If the 1.5 million node budget is reached, the result is labeled bounded search. The benchmark also includes a 14-stop heuristic example.

Houston / Sugar Land presets are fictional. Coordinates are illustrative miles, with no real addresses, road network, GPS, traffic, transit timetable, or field validation. Deadlines are completion deadlines; time windows do not span midnight.
